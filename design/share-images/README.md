# Share images

The extension uses the current Widgetify Mobile v2 designs for both 1080×1920 PNGs. The `.dc.html` files in `boards/` and PNGs in `reference/` document the previous proposal and do not depict the current output.

| Image | Current reference | What to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Mood of the month | [C7f](../../../widgetify-mobile-v2/design/v2/project/Mood-Share-Image.dc.html) | A white card, avatar on the top edge, seven glossy mood tiles per row, and the dominant mood's mascot and caption. The empty month uses the same layout with no dominant mood count. |
| Habit progress | [D11f](../../../widgetify-mobile-v2/design/v2/project/Yadkar-Sheet-Habit-Share-Image.dc.html) | A dark card with the user's profile, habit name and icon, a large streak and flame, and ten day beads per row for the current Jalali month. A zero streak fades the flame. |

Both images place their content inside the story safe area. The shared glow and logo keep them recognizable as a pair. The accent comes from the habit colour or the month's most logged mood so it identifies the data in the image. Vazirmatn keeps Persian labels and numbers consistent with mobile.

The extension keeps its own dialogs and Copy and Download actions. The mood dialog still lets you choose the month. The canvas only shows a completed image; failed preparations can be retried.
